## 🔎 On-Page SEO & Keyword Research

The foundation of every ranking page. I research **buyer-intent and low-competition keywords**, map them to the right pages, and optimize everything Google reads: meta titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, URL slugs, image ALT text, and internal linking. The result is content that matches **search intent** and earns clicks from the SERP, not just impressions.

## ⚙️ Technical SEO & Core Web Vitals

Great content can't rank on a broken site. I run **full technical SEO audits** and fix what's underneath: crawlability, indexing errors, **Google Search Console** issues, XML sitemaps, robots.txt, canonical tags, redirect chains, broken links, and 404s. I also handle **Core Web Vitals and page speed optimization** — LCP, INP, CLS — so your site loads fast and passes Google's page experience signals.

## 🤖 AEO & GEO — AI Search Optimization

Search is moving from ten blue links to AI-generated answers. I optimize for **featured snippets, People Also Ask, and voice search**, then go further with **GEO**: entity-based content structuring, **schema markup**, clean semantic architecture, and answer-first formatting that makes your brand quotable to **AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity**. This is where early movers win.

## 📍 Local SEO & Google Business Profile

For businesses that need customers nearby. I optimize your **Google Business Profile**, build consistent **NAP citations**, target local keywords, create location pages that actually rank, and push you into the **Google Map Pack** where local buying decisions happen.

## 🔗 Off-Page SEO & Link Building

Authority is still the strongest ranking signal. I build **white-hat backlinks** through guest posting, digital PR, quality business listings, and competitor backlink gap analysis — earning **domain authority** that compounds instead of disappearing in the next algorithm update.

## 🛒 WordPress, Shopify & Ecommerce SEO

Platform-specific optimization that generic advice misses: **Rank Math and Yoast configuration**, WooCommerce and Shopify product page SEO, collection and category optimization, faceted navigation control, and product schema for **rich results** in Google Shopping.
